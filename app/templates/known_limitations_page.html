Known Limitations

What is validated, what carries a methodology caveat, what is not yet validated, and what is not yet supported across Finco One projects.

Validated Models

Generic Solar and Generic Wind are validated against internal reference workbooks, not a full external bank model review. Core cost and revenue figures — CAPEX, Revenue, OPEX, EBITDA, IDC, bank fees, senior debt amount, realized gearing, and equity funding stack — agree with the reference workbook to well under 0.2%.

TUHO and Oborovo are held as fixed, validated reference projects and are used as a known-good anchor for testing the model; they are not editable in place (see the "Protected original" notice on those projects).

Methodology Caveats

  • Debt sizing proxy. Debt service shape, DSCR, and IRR figures carry a documented methodology caveat versus the reference workbooks. The runtime model's debt-sizing proxy differs structurally from the reference workbook's own proxy: senior debt service amortization shape can differ even though the total senior debt amount and gearing match the reference workbook closely.
  • Project IRR definition. Project IRR is calculated on an unlevered basis and can diverge from the reference workbook, which nets real interest expense through tax. This is a deliberate, documented modelling choice, not an error.
  • Scenario assumptions. Scenario comparisons (compare and multi-compare) are descriptive: they compare saved scenario snapshots and saved runtime summaries, not unsaved draft values. For projects without full Excel-parity validation, comparison results are illustrative and should not be used for external decisions.
  • DSCR methodology. Average and Minimum DSCR move in the expected direction when key assumptions change (debt margin, tenor, gearing, OPEX, generation), but exact DSCR figures inherit the same debt-sizing proxy caveat noted above and should be read as directionally reliable rather than bank-model-exact.
  • Sensitivity-testing observations. Stress testing key assumptions (cost, revenue, financing terms) moves results in the expected direction in almost all cases. One small, documented exception exists: for Generic Solar, a revenue increase produces a tiny (well under 0.1 percentage point) decrease in Equity IRR instead of an increase, even though every other metric improves as expected. This is flagged as an open methodology question, not hidden or silently accepted.

Not Yet Validated

The following configurations run and produce results, but have not been validated against a reference workbook:

  • BESS (Generic BESS)
  • Hybrid (Generic Solar + BESS, Generic Wind + BESS)
  • Portfolio (experimental, internal use only)

Not Yet Supported

  • Multi-lender debt — only a single senior debt instrument is modelled; no mezzanine, junior debt, or multi-lender syndication.
  • Advanced tax structures — beyond loss carryforward, complex tax equity or partnership structures are not modelled.
  • R99/R102 promoted workflow — R99/R102 are audit-only fields, presented for traceability only. They are not approved for runtime cash routing, distribution triggers, or SHL service decisions.
  • Advanced debt-sculpting and cash-sweep methods — experimental sculpting and sweep methods exist in the codebase for testing purposes but are not yet promoted for use in standard project runs.

Disclaimer: Outputs are financial modelling estimates and not legal, tax, accounting or investment advice.
