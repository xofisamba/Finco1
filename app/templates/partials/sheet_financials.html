{# ── Accounting Conventions (V4-1: explicit transparency) ── #}
Accounting Conventions
Opening Deficit Reconstruction The period-0 equity deficit is solved algebraically from Assets − Debt − SHL so the Balance Sheet closes exactly at COD. This avoids understating the opening equity shortfall for projects with pre-funded DSRA / MRA cash at COD (e.g. Oborovo).
Tax Payable Timing CIT is accrued in P&L on an accrual basis (annual) but collected as cash tax in H2 of each operating year (bridge convention). The timing difference accumulates as a tax payable balance embedded in Retained Earnings until settled.
SHL Principal — Notional Adjustment SHL principal repayments reduce the SHL balance in liabilities without a corresponding cash outflow (equity-contribution mechanics). The offsetting credit is carried through Retained Earnings as an equity value accretion, keeping the Balance Sheet closed.
